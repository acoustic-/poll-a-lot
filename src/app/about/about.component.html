About Poll-A-Lot

Poll-A-Lot helps groups pick what to watch: create a poll, add candidate movies or shows, share the link, and vote together.

It started as a small hobby project born out of one too many group chats stuck on "so what are we actually watching," and kept growing whenever I wanted to try something new.

How it works

  1. Create a poll and give it a name.
  2. Search TMDB and add movies or shows as candidates.
  3. Share the poll link with your group.
  4. Everyone votes, no account needed to take part.

Features

  • TMDB-powered search, cast/crew details, and streaming availability
  • Ratings from OMDb and content warnings from Does the Dog Die
  • AI-generated poll descriptions and movie suggestions (via Gemini)
  • Letterboxd integration: "already seen" badges, a profile panel with favourites and diary stats, and a Daily Reel Pace tracker
  • A personal watchlist, separate from any poll
  • Installable as an app, with a dark theme

Sign in with Google

You can vote and react to any poll without an account, just pick a nickname. Signing in with Google unlocks creating and managing your own polls, a watchlist that follows you across devices, and linking a Letterboxd account. It's the same Google sign-in used throughout the app: your email and login credentials are never stored by Poll-A-Lot.

Your data & privacy

A poll's movies and votes are visible to anyone with its link. Polls aren't listed or searchable, but the link itself isn't access-locked. If you're signed in, other voters see the display name and (optional) profile photo you set in Settings; you can change either at any time, or delete your public profile entirely, from there. Everything else, including your watchlist, region, and any linked Letterboxd account, is private to your own account. As a hobby project, nothing is guaranteed and data can be removed without notice. You've been warned! 😉
