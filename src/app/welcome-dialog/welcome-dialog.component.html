Welcome to Poll-a-Lot 🎬

This is a hobby project built by one person, so expect the occasional plot hole. Sign in with Google to unlock the director's cut: start your own polls, revisit the ones you've voted in, customize your profile, and keep a watchlist for later. No account? No problem, you can still vote and browse without signing in.

  • add Create your own polls
  • history Track polls you've voted in
  • settings Edit your profile & settings
  • bookmark_border Add movies to your watchlist

You'll sign in through Firebase Authentication. Your first name and photo are shown to other voters by default (editable in Settings), and your email and credentials are never stored.
