Welcome to Poll-a-Lot 🎬

A hobby project built by one person, so the occasional plot hole is part of the experience. Sign in with Google and unlock the director's cut: start your own polls, revisit the ones you've voted in, customize your profile, and keep a watchlist for later. No account, no problem — you can still vote and browse the whole time.

  • add Create your own polls
  • history Track polls you've voted in
  • settings Edit your profile & settings
  • bookmark_border Add movies to your watchlist

Firebase Authentication — first name & photo are shown to other voters by default (editable in Settings); email and credentials are never stored.
